Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Remli (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an offline reminder app for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your reminders and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Remli has no internet permission, so the app itself cannot send your data anywhere.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • If you email us feedback, your own email app sends only what you decide to send.

Information stored on your device

Remli stores the following in its private app storage on your phone:

  • Reminders you create: titles, notes, dates and times, repeat rules, checklists, tags, list/category, priority, chosen icon, sound and vibration, and any location text or link you type in.
  • Settings: accent colour, app icon, sounds, quiet-hours times, clock format and similar preferences, plus an optional first name used only for the greeting on the home screen.
  • Activity counts: how many reminders you completed per day, used only to show streaks and Insights inside the app.

This information never leaves your device through Remli. Android keeps each app’s private storage separate, so other apps cannot read it.

Device backups

Remli allows Android’s standard backup feature. If you have turned on device backup (for example Google backup on Google devices, or your phone maker’s backup service), Android may include Remli’s data in that backup. Your backup provider handles these backups under its own privacy policy. You can turn device backup off in your phone’s settings.

Information we collect

None. Remli does not collect personal information, and nothing you enter in the app is sent to us or to anyone else. The only time we receive anything is when you choose to email us (see “Feedback emails”).

Information we share

None. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and Remli contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Remli does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or the internet.

Feedback emails

If you choose Settings → About Remli → Feedback & Support and pick Suggest a Feature, Report a Bug or General Feedback, Remli opens your own email app with a prepared draft addressed to remli.support@gmail.com. The draft includes:

  • the app version and build number,
  • your Android version,
  • your device manufacturer (bug reports only) and device model.

You can read, change or delete any of this before sending, and nothing is sent unless you press send yourself. The draft never includes your reminders, contacts, location, phone number or device identifiers. If you send the email, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote. We use it only to reply to you and to improve Remli, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Your email provider’s own privacy policy applies to sending it.

Links to other services

Rate Remli opens the Google Play Store, and the online copy of this policy opens in your browser. Those services are run by their own providers under their own privacy policies.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete a reminder: open it and tap the delete button, or press and hold it on the home screen.
  • Delete finished reminders: Settings → Data → Clear completed.
  • Delete everything: uninstall Remli, or go to your phone’s Settings → Apps → Remli → Storage → Clear data.
  • Feedback emails: write to remli.support@gmail.com and we will delete any emails you have sent us.

Children

Remli is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new “Last updated” date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
