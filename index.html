01

Information stored on your device

Remli keeps the following in its private app storage on your phone:

  • Reminders you create: titles, notes, dates and times, repeat rules, checklists, tags, list/category, priority, sound and vibration choices, and any location text or link you type in.
  • Settings: theme (System, Light, Dark or AMOLED), accent colour, app icon, language (English, French or Dutch), sounds, quiet-hours times, clock format and similar preferences, plus an optional first name you can enter for the greeting on the home screen.
  • Activity counts: how many reminders you completed each day, used only to show streaks and Insights inside the app.
  • Dismissed suggestions: a short list of the smart suggestions you closed, so they don’t come back.

Android keeps each app’s private storage separate, so other apps cannot read it.

02

Smart features, done on your phone

  • Smart icons: when you type a title like “Dentist” or “Gym”, Remli matches it against a word list built into the app and shows a fitting icon.
  • Smart suggestions: simple rules built into the app look at your own reminders (for example, a one-off medicine reminder) and may offer to make it repeat. Nothing is applied until you tap it.
  • Sharing to Remli: when you share a message to Remli from another app (for example WhatsApp, Telegram or email), only that text is used to fill in a new reminder on your phone. Remli can’t read your chats or emails, and nothing is saved until you tap Set reminder.
  • Home-screen widget: if you add the “Remli · Today” widget, it shows today’s reminder titles and times on your home screen. Anyone who can see your home screen can see them, so leave it off if that matters to you.
  • Notifications and alarms: reminder titles can appear on your lock screen, depending on your phone’s notification settings.
03

Backups

Back up to a file

In Settings → Data → Back up to a file, Remli saves your reminders and settings into a single file. Android’s own file picker asks you where to put it, for example your Downloads folder or a cloud-drive app you already use. Remli never uploads it anywhere itself.

The backup file is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere you trust. If you save it to a cloud-drive app, that provider’s own privacy policy applies to the file.

Restore from a file reads a Remli backup you pick and replaces the reminders and settings on this phone. Remli asks you to confirm first.

Device backups

Remli allows Android’s standard backup feature. If you have turned on device backup (for example Google backup, or your phone maker’s backup service), Android may include Remli’s data in it. Your backup provider handles that under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

06

Feedback emails

If you choose Settings → About Remli → Feedback & Support and pick Suggest a Feature, Report a Bug or General Feedback, Remli opens your own email app with a prepared draft addressed to remli.support@gmail.com. The draft includes:

  • the app version and build number,
  • your Android version,
  • your device manufacturer (bug reports only) and device model.

You can read, change or delete any of this before sending, and nothing is sent unless you press send yourself. The draft never includes your reminders, contacts, location, phone number or device identifiers. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to reply and to improve Remli, and delete it when no longer needed.

08

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete a reminder: open it and tap the delete button, or press and hold it on the home screen.
  • Delete finished reminders: Settings → Data → Clear completed.
  • Delete a backup file: delete it in your Files app, or wherever you saved it.
  • Delete everything: uninstall Remli, or go to your phone’s Settings → Apps → Remli → Storage → Clear data.
  • Feedback emails: write to remli.support@gmail.com and we will delete any emails you have sent us.
